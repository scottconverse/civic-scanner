# Other modes

All modes use `editorial-controls.md`, exact source attribution, and explicit access limits. Use the host's available search and file tools; no tool name is assumed.

## `verify-only {claim}`

State the claim precisely, including the proposed action, date, body, and stage. Find the best Tier A record and its exact location. Seek contradictory or later records, inspect a recording at the relevant timestamp if the claim is about a meeting action, and run the adversarial checks. Output `VERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `FALSE` with the evidence and the smallest wording that the record supports. Do not upgrade an agenda listing into a vote.

## `research {topic}`

Find the official record chain and affected jurisdictions. Build a dated chronology and explain the policy mechanism, people affected, money or land involved, remaining decisions, and competing accounts. Deliver a research brief with verified facts, disputed claims, missing documents, suggested records requests, interviews, and a falsification plan. If the topic involves a meeting, apply the meeting coverage rules in `daily-scan.md` to that meeting.

## `legal-threat {situation}`

This is issue spotting for an editor, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel. Identify jurisdiction and deadline, preserve exact notices or correspondence, distinguish a records-access dispute from a publication threat, and identify potential defamation, privacy, privilege, fair-report, and anti-SLAPP questions. Cite current primary legal sources when available and recommend qualified local counsel for high-stakes decisions. Do not claim privilege or immunity without a source-specific legal basis.

## `discover {city, state}`

Create a source registry using `source-template.md`. Identify the city council agenda and packet portal, minutes, official recording/video channel and transcript availability, boards and commissions, county and school district, relevant special districts, public records request channel, established local news outlets, and community signal venues. Verify official identity and URLs from the government's site. Record jurisdiction, typical posting frequency, access method, and last checked date. Do not claim a portal has searchable transcripts until tested. Keep Tier B/C separate. If a source is blocked, preserve its URL and access status; do not infer contents from snippets. Use the new registry for a small dry-run scan, then mark unverified entries for follow-up.

What It Does

Replaces 4-10 hours of morning newsroom work with a structured, verified pipeline.

Scans Public Records

Checks government agendas, meeting minutes, budgets, permits, and official YouTube channels. Classifies every source by tier (A/B/C) and extracts actionable civic data.

Kills Bad Stories

Four kill gates can stop a story before it reaches an editor. No Tier A source? Killed. Counter-narrative stronger? Suppressed. Below newsworthiness threshold? Held.

Guides the Reporter

Every story produces a Reporter Task Memo: what's confirmed, what's missing, who to call, what documents to file, and what evidence would kill the story.

Packages for Distribution

SEO headlines, social media posts for 4 platforms, newsletter briefs, email subject lines, visual direction briefs with AI generation prompts — all per story.

Remembers Across Runs

Beat memory tracks story threads over time. Score trends show if stories are gaining or losing newsworthiness. Suppression triggers are monitored every run.

Works in Any City

Discovery mode auto-builds a source registry for any US municipality. Finds agenda portals, YouTube channels, school boards, newspapers, and community forums.

Severity Coding

Every story carries a severity color through the entire pipeline.

GREEN Clean pass. No caveats. Ready for reporter follow-up.
AMBER Contested. Both sides required. Framing adjusted.
RED Killed. Counter-narrative won or unattributable claims.
GRAY Held. Awaiting documents or below threshold.

8 Mandatory Controls

Non-negotiable. Every mode, every story, every claim.

1. Hard-No-Bluff

No Tier A source = no publication. Period.

2. Source Tiers

A: publishable. B: leads only. C: signals, never cite.

3. Adversarial Verification

4 gates: contestation, adverse search, counter-narrative, self-referential.

4. De-Escalation

"Scheduled to" not "will." Fact/interpretation/allegation separated.

5. Suppression Ledger

Dead stories with reopen triggers. A pipeline, not a graveyard.

6. Dynamic Filtering

Tier-specific search domains and keep/discard criteria.

7. Blocked Source Intel

Blocked results become leads, not dead ends.

8. Beat Memory

Thread tracking across runs. Score trends. Trigger monitoring.

## City Profile

- **City:** Colorado Springs, Colorado
- **County:** El Paso County
- **Population:** ~490,000 (2nd largest city in Colorado)
- **Government Type:** Strong Mayor–Council (adopted 2010, implemented 2011)
- **Mayor:** Yemi Mobolade (nonpartisan, since 2023)
- **Council:** 9 members — 6 district, 3 at-large; 4-year terms, 2-term limit
- **State Open Records Law:** CORA (Colorado Open Records Act, CRS §24-72-200.1 et seq.)
  - Response deadline: 3 business days (may extend 7 business days for good cause)
  - Fees: Reasonable costs for research and retrieval
  - Appeal: District court

## Notes

- Colorado Springs uses **Legistar** for legislative tracking and **Granicus** for meeting video — both are major platforms with good data access
- City Code is on **American Legal Publishing** (not Municode)
- No dedicated YouTube channel for council meetings — video is via Granicus and SpringsTV
- **Colorado Springs Utilities** is a city enterprise (not a separate district) — Council sets rates
- El Paso County BoCC meets at Centennial Hall downtown, same area as City Hall
- D11 is the primary school district; Academy District 20 and others also serve parts of the city
- The Gazette may be behind a paywall — apply Blocked Source Intelligence Protocol if crawl-blocked
- District 2 Council seat is vacant as of March 2026 (applications closed 3/26/2026)
